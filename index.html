// Journey

My Tech Journey

2020
First Custom ROM Flash
Flashed my first custom ROM — the moment I discovered that I could completely transform what my device could do. It was the spark that ignited my passion for tech.
2021
Virtual Machine Experimentation
Began experimenting with virtual machines, running multiple operating systems simultaneously and exploring different computing environments.
2022
Hackintosh & Linux Distros
Started hackintoshing and trying out several Linux distros — Ubuntu and Debian were the first. Fell in love with open-source software and the freedom it offers.
2023
Daily Driving Linux & Major Milestones
A massive year: daily drove Linux, installed Windows 11 on a Poco X3 Pro smartphone, achieved a successful hackintosh build, got my first ever actual Mac (M2 Mac Mini), and downgraded an iPhone 3GS to 3.1.2 using Cydia SHSH blobs.
2024
Wallpaper Contest, Arch Linux & GSI Ports
Made my first wallpaper for the Ubuntu 24.04 wallpaper contest (didn't win though), installed Arch Linux, and started trying out GSI ports on my Redmi K20 Pro.
2025
Viral Growth & Major Contributions
Fixed multitouch in an iOS 7 port for the 4th generation iPod touch, started blowing up on YouTube Shorts and TikTok, began contributing to the AppleInternal community, booted native Fedora Workstation on my Poco X3 Pro with a mainline Linux kernel, began experimenting with AOSP builds on my Samsung Galaxy S2, and made the somewhat viral meme "fun fact: this ipad is older than u".
2026
AOSP Builds & UTM Virtualization
Successfully built Android 4.2.2 from AOSP source code for my Google Nexus 10, and successfully booted KDE Debian on my M5 iPad Pro using UTM with JIT — pushing the limits of what mobile hardware can do.

About Me

I'm Cidstech — a tech enthusiast who believes no device should be left behind. My journey started with flashing my first custom ROM in 2020 and has evolved into a passion for pushing hardware to its absolute limits.

From building AOSP ROMs for the Google Nexus 10 to fixing multitouch in an iOS 7 port for the iPod touch 4G, to booting KDE Debian on an M5 iPad Pro — I've always been obsessed with making old tech do new things.

Now, I'm sharing that journey with thousands through TechTok content and Tech Shorts, helping others discover the magic of custom development, jailbreaking, and the beauty of legacy tech.
